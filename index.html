01 — Welcome

Four decades of service… and counting

Thank you for visiting Kohler Architects Inc. — a showcase for our work and four decades of service to the Mid-Peninsula.

Here you’ll find the range and quality of our practice. Every project is shaped around the needs and wishes of the individual client.

Big enough to deliver complete architectural service. Small enough that every client gets the principals’ personal attention. Please contact us if we can be of service.

02 — Professional Services

Complete service, from concept to completion of construction

I

Project Initiation, Design & Support

From the first consultation, we manage every aspect of design — site, plans, and massing — and coordinate the full consultant team: civil, structural, energy, landscape, interior, and lighting.

II

Construction Documents

Complete construction documents: site, grading & drainage, Title 24, plans, elevations, sections, electrical, plumbing, foundation, and framing — prepared right, the first time.

III

Approvals & Entitlements

We guide design review and permitting through public agencies. Roger served on Palo Alto’s Historic Resources Board for over 25 years — three times as chair — so your project moves through approvals with confidence.

IV

Complete Project

We stay involved through construction — reviewing plans and observing the work as it is built, so construction stays faithful to the design.

06 — The Kohler Team

Our Team

Led by founder Roger Kohler and his son Matt, the firm is firmly established as one of the leading architectural firms on the Mid-Peninsula — fluent in Chinese, German, and Farsi.

Roger Kelley Kohler AIA

Founder & Principal Architect

CA C-7334 · OR O-2326 · HI AR-14093

After working with recognized local architects, Roger opened his own office in 1980 specializing in residential architecture. The firm has designed hundreds of new homes — many in Palo Alto — plus remodels, multi-family, commercial, and historic projects.

University of Oregon · B.Arch 1968 · Licensed 1973

Matthew Kohler AIA

CEO & Principal Architect · Joined 2001

CA C-38100 · Licensed 2019

Matt began as his father’s apprentice. Now a licensed architect and CEO, he leads day-to-day operations as principal designer — known for high-level design sensibility and attentive client service.

California Architectural Licensing Program · 2019

Jeff Kuo

Architect · Joined 1990

CA C-27349 · Licensed 1997

A creative designer with extensive experience serving both individual clients and builders, Jeff leads a wide range of projects from concept through construction drawings.

Washington University, St. Louis · M.Arch 1990

Uli Dueregger

Uli prepares high-quality production drawings — and especially enjoys elevations — with carefully assembled permit packages that earn clients timely approvals. A Certified Green Building Professional since 2008, she also guides green product choices.
